# HereO demo narration (v5)

Voice: warm, calm, confident narrator (ElevenLabs, multilingual v2 or newer, stability ~0.5, style ~0.3).
Rae's in-app line: a natural British woman's voice, a little breathless, not theatrical.
Aim for about 200 words over 2:12, leaving the stings and key screen moments room to breathe.
Each line starts at its beat. Trim a line rather than speed it up.

| Beat | Starts at | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 0:01 (after the fanfare peak) | Most people walk home with their keys between their fingers. HereO is for that walk. |
| 01 Hold for help | beat start | Rae feels followed. She holds one button for two seconds. Silently. |
| 02 A plan in seconds | beat start | Meta's Muse Spark reads the situation and plans the whole response in a single turn: who to send, what each person should do, and when to call 999. |
| 03 Nia gets the call | as the ring plays | Nia is a verified hero two minutes away. Her phone rings. |
| 04 Rae says what's happening | after Rae's line has played and the transcript appears | Muse Voice turns Rae's words into text in real time, and the coordinator updates everyone's plan. |
| 05 One clear job | beat start | Nia gets one clear, safe job. Never confront. Just be there. |
| 06 The photo | beat start | Rae shares where she is. SAM 3.1 blurs every face and number plate before anyone sees it. The original is never stored. |
| 07 Where to look | beat start | Muse Spark reads the street, and tells Nia exactly where to find her. |
| 08 Nia arrives | beat start | Help arrives in minutes, from someone who lives nearby. |
| 09 Rae is safe | beat start | Rae is safe. And she was never alone. |
| End card | under the soar | HereO. Neighbours who show up. Launching in Cambridge. |
