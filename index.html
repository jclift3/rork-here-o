Why HereO

Fear keeps people home. Neighbours can change that.

Too many people plan their evenings around the walk back. Emergency services are vital, but the first minutes matter, and the closest help is usually a person a few doors away. HereO makes it easy for that person to show up.

Muse Dispatcher · built with Meta AI

An AI dispatcher that keeps everyone calm and clear.

In a crisis nobody wants to type. Muse Dispatcher uses Meta's Muse models to turn a hold, a whispered sentence or a photo of the scene into a plan: who goes where, what to say, and when to call emergency services.

Every plan passes through safety guardrails first. It never tells anyone to confront, chase or put themselves at risk.

For guardians

Being a hero is mostly just showing up.

You don't need a cape. You need to be nearby, be visible and be kind. HereO tells you exactly what to do, and nothing more.

Be present.

Stand where you can be seen. Presence alone changes what happens next.

Don't pursue.

You are there for the person, not the threat. No chasing, no confronting.

Call 911.

If there's danger, emergency services come first. HereO fills the minutes until they arrive.
