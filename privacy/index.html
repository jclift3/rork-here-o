Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Applies to: the HereO app for iOS and Android, hereo.app, and our emails.

HereO helps people get help fast from the people nearby and the people who love them. To do that we have to handle some of the most sensitive information there is: where you are, and that you might be in trouble. We take that seriously. This policy explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • We use your location to get you help, to show your circle your trip, and (only if you volunteer as a hero) to match you with someone nearby who needs help.
  • When you ask for help, the people helping you see what they need to find you and nothing more. Nearby heroes never see your name.
  • We never sell your data, we never show ads, and we never share your location with anyone you haven't chosen or who isn't helping you at that moment.
  • Photos you send during a help request have faces and number plates blurred before anyone sees them. Voice recordings are turned into text and the audio is thrown away.
  • You can see, download or delete your data at any time from the app or by emailing [privacy@hereo.app].

HereO is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call 999 in the UK, 911 in the US, or 112 elsewhere.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Members: anyone with a HereO account.
  • Askers: members who start a help request, a Trip or a check-in.
  • Circle members: people an asker has added to their circle (family, partners, friends).
  • Heroes: members who volunteer to respond to nearby help requests, at any level (Neighbour, Guardian, Her-O, or partner responder such as a college porter or security officer).
  • Website visitors and people on our waitlist.
  • Other people who appear in information a member gives us, for example someone in the background of a scene photo or a person described in a help request.

3.3 Help requests

When you ask for help (by holding the button, tapping, ringing nearby, or by missing a check-in), we create a record of that request. It can include:

  • the time, how it was started, your location, and whether your alarm was loud or silent;
  • anything you type, say or show, such as a short note, messages, a voice note or a photo;
  • an assessment of how urgent it is and a short summary for the people helping ("the brief"), written by our AI assistant or by a simple rule-based fallback;
  • who was alerted, who responded, how far away they were, and what they did ("on my way", "with you", "released");
  • whether the request was closed normally, ended with your duress PIN, or reported as a false alarm;
  • thank-you notes exchanged afterwards.

Why: to get you help, to keep the people helping you up to date, to keep a record in case something goes wrong, to investigate misuse, and to make HereO safer.

Lawful basis (UK): vital interests (protecting your life or someone else's) and contract. 3.4 Photos and voice notes during a help request

  • Photos. If you send a photo of where you are, our system first finds and blurs every face and number plate. If that blurring step isn't available, the photo is not shared at all. Only the blurred photo is stored, privately, and only the people taking part in that help request can see it. Our AI assistant then describes the place (shop names, signs, junctions) to help heroes find you. It is instructed never to describe people.
  • Voice notes. If you record a voice note, we turn it into text and add the text to your help request. The audio itself is not stored.

Today these models are provided by Anthropic (Claude) and Meta (Muse Spark, SAM and speech transcription). [LAWYER/JACOB: confirm which providers are live at launch.]

  • Our providers process this data on our behalf under contracts that forbid them from using it to train their models. [LAWYER: verify each provider's commercial terms and zero/limited retention settings.]

We keep a log of what the AI decided and why for each help request, so we can review it and make HereO safer.

8. How we protect your data

  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Database access rules mean each person can only see what they are allowed to see. For example, nearby heroes cannot see the asker's name.
  • PINs are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Photos are blurred before they are stored, and stored privately.
  • Only a small number of named staff can access personal data, and access is logged.
  • We will tell you and the ICO (or other regulators where required) if a breach puts your rights at risk.

No system is perfectly secure. If you think your account has been compromised, contact us straight away.
